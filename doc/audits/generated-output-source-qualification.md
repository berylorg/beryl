## Corrections And Limits

Review removed extra full-page encoding/decoding copies, rejected oversized reader-tag payloads
before allocation and redacted pending bytes from owner diagnostics. Automatic terminal receipts
retain their admitted reservation through an explicit final acknowledgement. Native reset/drain
and fast-exit startup-proof corrections are preserved in the
[closure failure record](../failures/generated-reader-closure.md).

UNC policy acceptance is tested; live SMB access was not exercised. The qualified kernel/runtime
envelope and original-owner closure requirements remain those of the
[supervisor contract](../../crates/beryl-wsl-supervisor/doc/design.md).
Raw task logs/manifests were temporary execution material; this audit retains their identities and
outcomes. All thirteen exact consumer fixture paths were verified absent, and the exact owned
execution directory was reclaimed with `cleanup-dir.exe` after review. No Cargo/native test owner
remains active. Shared canonical and Cargo artifacts remain available for the next phase.
