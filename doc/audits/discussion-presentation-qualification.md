## Boundary

This qualification covers the [discussion presentation contract](../../crates/beryl-app/doc/design-discussion-presentation.md)
and [feature-owned status strip](../features/branch-discussions/gui.md#discussion-status-strip).
It mounts the existing segmented status bar, coherent composer contribution, bounded persistent
notice and explicit same-job Retry through the original published service graph.

The observation joins durable discussion/job/parent facts with bounded process-local admission,
Collision and outcome custody. Snapshots retain weak service/Home identities and exact local/Home
intervals rather than jobs, resolution text, storage handles or GUI owners. Local registry mutation
participates in the same revision election as publication, including the pre-job admission interval.
Weak audit slots are inspected without upgrading or dropping their final owner under the registry
mutex. Existing configured attempt capacity supplies the bound.

Initial, restored, ordinary and recovered selection transport carries one prepared status/composer
tuple. Recovered publication validates the complete resident cohort, then elects local state,
original CAS/process admission, exact selection ownership and the observed Home interval before
pure prepared release. Busy/stale refusal preserves original resident, claim and failure custody.
The established CAS-to-process-to-registry order also covers concurrent reservation destruction.

Exact candidate-session allocation is unpublished preparation. Structurally valid Pending and
Archived discussions can allocate/read/dispose their unchanged editor session; actual edits,
history adoption, publication, input and steering remain independently gated. No new session kind,
storage family, alternate composer or mutation permission was introduced.

## Corrections And Reuse

Earlier diagnostic invocations are not represented as wholly passing. The elected GUI apply first
deadlocked by requerying Home health; pure prepared application fixed that boundary. Restored
startup then exposed the overbroad session-opening edit guard, confirmed by the original typed
`DiscussionMutationBlocked` refusal. The final native packet above qualifies both corrections.
The recovery fixture now mounts the real status observer while preserving its manually driven Exit
protocol, and the Retry fixture fences/drains original process work only during raw setup before
coherently reopening for the actual GUI action.

Shared exact-CAS fixtures now complete the real bounded context seed and preserve the immutable
source profile. Inherited context authenticates the original turn owner separately from the logical
selected thread. Activation uses the qualified basis's selected path after queue changes; disposal
names the published checkpoint rather than its private session fork. The final handoff run qualifies
the corrected inherited and readonly fixtures. Lasting mechanics are recorded in
[the presentation failure record](../failures/discussion-presentation.md).

The recovery probe now captures completion, current service presence, job and generation under one
owner borrow, then compares that generation with the separate exact-release tuple. Temporary
service transfer waits rather than invoking a getter that requires GUI ownership.

The broad run's passing small cases cover the affected notice branches: `marker_limit_feedback`
preserves capacity refusal and exact routing; `marker_storage_refusal` and
`image_paste_storage_refusal` preserve storage refusal and the editor; `marker_preparation_failure`
preserves persistent exact-request feedback. The `mutation_completion` cases cover intermediate
admitted-work uncertainty, committed successor/cleanup failures, original custody and retirement.
The two positive large controls add the size-scale edit/history and clipboard/cut/undo paths.
No size-dependent production logic changed. Ordinary Live gate computation is unchanged by the
final lifecycle correction; focused Fencing/resume tests supply its separate evidence.

The excluded 60-second controls are large clipboard size and storage refusal, and large draft
capacity, storage and ambiguous refusal. They are ancillary size controls for this phase, not
passing cases or proof of a regression. Complete-target or general large-draft performance
acceptance is not claimed.

## Evidence Limits

The native status matrix uses prepared descriptive states with real identity fences. Separate owning
service cases supply durable/local state evidence. Mounted Retry starts from an admitted fixture job;
the Archived first-frame fixture reaches archive through typed settlement and canonical terminal
events. Neither substitutes for actual resolution-tool transport followed by ordinary parent
execution. That complete mounted chain, restart cuts and aggregate discussion checkpoint remain
the next plan boundary. This qualification adds no live-model, GPU paint, stress or performance
claim. Prior context and non-GUI handoff audits retain their original scope.
