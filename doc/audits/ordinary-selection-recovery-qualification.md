# Ordinary Selection Recovery Qualification

## Boundary

Qualification is accepted. This record covers original ordinary thread selection through
process-owned same-home recovery in surviving native windows, including final shutdown of
delivered Page custody. Visible Thread Switcher mounting remains a separate acceptance boundary.

Controlling contracts are [ordinary selection recovery](../systems/backend-runtime/design.md#interrupted-ordinary-thread-selection-during-same-home-recovery),
[shell capture](../../crates/beryl-app/doc/design-shell-lifecycle.md#failed-home-ordinary-selection-recovery),
[composer custody](../../crates/beryl-app/doc/design-catalog-and-composer.md#range-backed-composer-host)
and [detached final shutdown](../../crates/beryl-app/doc/design-catalog-and-composer.md#detached-read-only-composer).
State candidate claims and Syndic candidate-history reads retain their owning contracts.

## Verified Behavior

The actual ordinary operation retains original prepared claim and save witnesses, receipt and
reconciliation custody. Recovery settles original publication before original claim outcome and
qualifies exact prior or replacement claims under fresh candidate authority. Original uncertainty
retains unavailable intent and duplicate exclusion. Recovery neither repeats the original save or
claim nor substitutes creation-specific permission.

Original workers and deliveries drain before capture. Native reservations, selection leases,
protected prior edits/history, unpublished-target cleanup, completed canonical disposal and original
Page obligations remain under their original owners. Unchanged windows retain strict capture and
source uniqueness checks. Fresh nonempty editor/transcript bindings and renewed close custody
precede atomic whole-graph publication in the same HWND.

Source-owned cleanup capsules preserve immutable original environment, session, key, token and
ledger facts without retaining provider, Home, service or dispatch authority. Complete source-set
qualification precedes transfer; the whole group is retained before fallible actual driver stop and
exclusive service/store conversion. Genuine worker destruction is separate from cancellation
admission. Same-owner return preserves original custody through checked widget rebinding.

Healthy final Exit has a distinct exact close/flush/selected/installed-source qualification. The
same enabled read-only editor survives live-service retirement. Actual native destruction precedes
release of the retained mount Entities; genuine widget custody destruction makes original tokens
ready. The retained original draft requires positive acknowledgements before environment release
and final completion. Failed-Home permissions, early widget disposal and synthetic readiness are
not completion evidence.

The shared nonfinal close consumer retains the same qualified group after its actual settled
Destroyed outcome. It performs exact retained-editor release and bounded positive acknowledgements
before releasing the original draft and environment; survivor and unresolved outcomes preserve
their existing recovery custody.
