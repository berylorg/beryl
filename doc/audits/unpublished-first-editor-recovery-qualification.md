# Accepted Boundary

Private same-home recovery can qualify an unpublished first editor's original opening and dispose
that exact fresh editor after the old runtime resources retire. Syndic accepts borrowed candidate
access for opening qualification and fresh abandonment preparation/classification, sharing the
ordinary abandonment algorithms and unchanged opening-history checks. Durable codecs and mutation
formats are unchanged.

The app captures a move-only cleanup intent containing immutable home/path, opening/disposal
identities and original outcome custody. It contains no old Home service, storage handle, composer
host/service, widget or publication lease. The separate reservation wrapper retains the existing
window and refuses ordinary unpublished-shell transfer until cleanup completes. Prepared services
must retire through their existing canonical resource boundary before capture.

Recovery settles original opening/disposal reconciliation before any fresh disposal command. Fresh
disposal retains its own receipt or reconciliation handle; only exact noncommit permits another
attempt with the original cleanup identity. Foreign homes/paths, dirty or occupied editors,
substituted openings, contradictory receipts and collisions cannot release the cleanup fence.
Complete cleanup is authenticated again when consumed by another recovery candidate.
